How Private Dermatology Pricing Is Typically Structured

Patient guide · 5 min read

Private dermatology costs in London are rarely a single flat figure, and understanding the typical structure helps avoid surprises once a consultation and treatment plan are underway.

The initial consultation fee

Most practices charge a set fee for the first consultation, which typically covers history-taking and examination but not additional tests or a treatment itself. This fee varies by consultant seniority and clinic, and is usually quoted upfront when booking.

Diagnostic tests are usually billed separately

A biopsy, patch test, or bloodwork ordered during a consultation is typically billed as a separate line item, in addition to the consultation fee itself, since the lab processing and analysis involve their own separate cost.

Treatment costs vary the most

Treatment pricing has the widest range of any component — a prescription-based plan costs considerably less than a course of laser treatment or injectable procedures, and getting a treatment-specific estimate only makes sense once a diagnosis and plan actually exist.

Follow-up appointments

Some practices bundle a certain number of follow-ups into an initial package; others bill each visit separately. Clarifying this upfront avoids assuming a review appointment is included when it isn't.

Getting a clear estimate before committing

A transparent practice should be able to give a reasonable cost range for likely tests and common treatments during the initial contact, even before an exact diagnosis is confirmed.
